Indraneel Bhattacharya

Astrologer, horoscope consultant and researcher, Chariot Sage Astrology Lab

Indraneel has studied astrology since the age of 13, and has spent the years since building that early study into a disciplined professional practice.

His consultancy focuses on high-stakes personal and professional turning points, including founder break-ups and complex UHNI and HNI family disputes.

His work has supported families navigating reunification and divorce, as well as founders facing consequential business transitions, through specialised horoscope design and interpretive and predictive analysis.

Within the Chariot Sage Astrology Lab, he researches on traditional astrological methods and challenges them with disciplined, modern interpretation to the circumstances behind each decision.

Each engagement is shaped around the people, relationships and choices involved whether the aim is to understand a separation, consider the possibility of reunification, or find direction through a difficult transition.

Consulting

Every consulting begins with the chart: what it shows, and what question it is being asked to answer.

  • Specialised horoscope design

    Multiple specialised horoscopes are prepared for each client, researched and decoded using a method developed over years of study.

  • Interpretive and Predictive Analysis

    A detailed reading of the chart for continued support through thick and thin.

  • Compatibility and timing

    Reading two charts together, or choosing a favourable time for an important step.

How an engagement works

  1. Discuss

    We discuss your goals, concerns, and the specific aspects of your life you want to explore through the chart.

  2. Tenure

    A tenure is decided, and I work with you through that stretch of decisions.

  3. Design

    Multiple specialised horoscopes are prepared, researching and decoding your thinking in detail.

  4. Support

    Guidance drawn on as needed through the tenure, as circumstances change.
