The map beneath the moment

An exploration of the relationship between intention, emotion and the patterns through which we make sense of our lives.

Astropsychology approaches a birth chart as a symbolic map for examining patterns of motivation, perception and response. Rather than treating a placement as an isolated verdict, it considers how different parts of the chart interact and how those patterns may be experienced in everyday life.

The purpose of an editorial inquiry is to make room for reflection. It asks what may be driving an intention, what emotional needs accompany it, and how a person interprets the circumstances in which a decision is made.

These pages bring together observations and perspectives from Chariot Sage. Each article is an invitation to examine an idea carefully, consider its context and arrive at a more informed point of view.
